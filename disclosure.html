Vulnerability Disclosure

Found a vulnerability? Good. Tell us first.

We teach security, so we’d better practice it. This policy explains how to report a vulnerability and what you can expect from us.

Scope

In scope:

  • The website and platform at [DOMAIN] and its subdomains.
  • The platform’s APIs.
  • The open-source repository.

Out of scope:

  • Lab and CTF targets. They’re vulnerable on purpose. The exception: if you escape a lab’s isolation, we absolutely want to know.
  • Denial of service attacks.
  • Social engineering or phishing against our team or users.
  • Third-party services we use, such as our hosting provider.
  • Automated scanner reports without a demonstrated impact.

How to report

Email security@[DOMAIN] and include:

  • A description of the vulnerability.
  • Steps to reproduce it.
  • The impact you were able to demonstrate.
  • How we can contact you.

While researching, please don’t access or modify other users’ data beyond what’s needed to prove the issue, and don’t disclose it publicly until we’ve fixed it.

What we commit to

  • Acknowledge your report within [N] business days.
  • Send an initial assessment within [N] business days.
  • Keep you updated until it’s fixed.
  • Credit you publicly, if you want.

Safe harbor

If you act in good faith and follow this policy, we will consider your research authorized and will not take legal action against you.
