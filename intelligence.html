How a mission works

Every lab follows the same cycle as a real incident. The only difference is that here, mistakes are cheap.

  1. 01

    Briefing

    You get what a real analyst gets: an alert, a vague ticket and, if you’re lucky, a nervous email from management.

  2. 02

    Investigation

    Logs, packet captures and memory dumps. Some of it matters, most of it doesn’t. Figuring out which is the job.

  3. 03

    Response

    Contain, eradicate and recover. Or exploit, pivot and persist, depending on your side. The clock keeps running either way.

  4. 04

    Debrief

    A breakdown of what you did, what you missed and how a senior would have approached it. This is where the learning sticks.

What makes it real

  • Real malware, safely isolated

    Live samples run in sandboxed networks that never touch your machine or the internet. Read how we isolate them

  • Noise on purpose

    False positives, irrelevant logs and red herrings. Because that’s what a real SOC queue looks like.

  • Time pressure

    Incidents escalate while you work. You learn to prioritize, not just to solve.

  • Research is part of the job

    We don’t hand you the answer. We teach you where to look: documentation, CVE databases, OWASP resources and source code.

  • Both sides of the fight

    Red, blue and purple. Understanding the attacker makes you a better defender, and the other way around.

  • Mistakes have consequences

    Break production in a lab and you’ll see the impact. Better here than on a Monday at work.
