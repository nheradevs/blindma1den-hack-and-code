Formats

Different formats train different muscles. Most people are only good at one.

  • Jeopardy

    A board of challenges by category and difficulty. Pick your battles, manage your time, and don’t fall in love with the 500-point one.

  • Attack-Defense

    Every team gets the same vulnerable services. Patch yours, exploit theirs. Both at the same time.

  • King of the Hill

    Take a machine and hold it. Everyone else is trying to kick you out, and they know the same tricks.

  • Incident Response

    Our own format: a live incident with logs, noise and a deadline. Points for what you find, and for what you contain.

The rules

Few rules, strictly enforced. Breaking one means disqualification.

  1. Attack the challenges, never the platform infrastructure.
  2. No sharing flags or solutions during the event, not even with friends on other teams.
  3. No brute-forcing the flag submission. If you need to guess, you haven’t solved it.
  4. Found a real bug in the platform? Report it through our disclosure policy. That earns you more respect than a flag.
  5. One person, one account. Squads are declared before the event starts.
