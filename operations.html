Certified isn’t the same as ready

Most teams find out how prepared they are during their first real incident. That’s an expensive way to learn.

  • Certificates expire in practice

    A certification proves someone passed an exam once. It doesn’t show how they react at 2 a.m. with incomplete logs.

  • You can’t practice on production

    Your team needs somewhere to break things, make mistakes and try again. Your real network isn’t that place.

  • Skills fade between incidents

    If the last real response was a year ago, the muscle memory is gone. Regular practice keeps it.
