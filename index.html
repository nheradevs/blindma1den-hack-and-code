Where do you start?

  • Social Engineering

    Forget firewalls. Bypass the one between the ears. Craft lures that hook execs and own the human layer.

  • Web App Exploits

    Way beyond alert(1) boxes. Break “patched” stacks and abuse the logic flaws the WAF never sees coming.

  • Network Pivoting

    The perimeter is a lie. Pivot from a lobby IoT camera to the Domain Controller. Map the forest, then burn it.

    EDR vs. LOLBins. Chain stealthy shells or hunt their signatures. Either way, it’s a game of shadows.

    Escape pods, abuse IAM over-permissions and own the fleet before the admin finishes their coffee.

  • Malware & Forensics

    Reverse the un-reversible. Decrypt ransomware and rebuild the logs someone tried very hard to burn.

  • Honeypots & Deception

    Flip the script. Build the trap, lure the threat and turn their exploit into your intel.

  • IoT & Mobile Carnage

    From Android rootkits to Zigbee hijacks. Turn “smart” convenience into your personal botnet.

  • GRC & Auditing

    Practice for the breach, not the checkbox. Prove your resilience before a $10M fine does it for you.

    One sweaty session. Attack, detect and mitigate in real time. Friction builds muscle.
