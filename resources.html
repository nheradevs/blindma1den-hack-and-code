Where to look when nobody tells you.

Half of this job is knowing where to search. These are the references we use every day, and the ones our labs expect you to find on your own.

OWASP projects

Free, community-built and used across the industry.

  • OWASP Top 10 ↗

    The most critical web application security risks. The starting point for everyone.

  • Cheat Sheet Series ↗

    Short, practical guides on specific topics, from authentication to deserialization.

  • Web Security Testing Guide ↗

    A complete methodology for testing web applications.

  • ASVS ↗

    The Application Security Verification Standard. What “secure enough” means, requirement by requirement.

  • Juice Shop ↗

    An intentionally insecure web app. Good for warming up before our labs.

References you’ll keep open

  • MITRE ATT&CK ↗

    Attacker tactics and techniques, mapped from real incidents. Useful for both sides.

  • NVD ↗

    The US National Vulnerability Database. Where you look up a CVE.

  • GTFOBins ↗

    Unix binaries that can be abused to escalate privileges or bypass restrictions.

  • LOLBAS ↗

    The Windows equivalent: legitimate binaries attackers use to stay under the radar.
